Applications

One opening, four jobs to do.

Every opening on a home or storefront faces the same four problems. Alutech builds a product line that answers each one, and most of our systems answer several at once.

Hurricane and storm protection

Alutech United's hurricane shutters give you peace of mind and protect your home and business from hurricane and storm damage. Every system is tested to the Florida Building Code, Texas Department of Insurance, Miami-Dade and International Building Code requirements.

See hurricane protection products

Insect protection

Our insect screening products keep flying insects and mosquitoes out. Roll down screens are retractable, manual or motorized, and cover French doors, lanais up to 25 feet wide, garage doors, outdoor kitchens and pool houses.

See insect protection products

Security and protection

Most intrusions happen through windows and doors. Securing these vulnerable points of entry should be your number one priority in break-in prevention. Roll shutters and security grilles lock from the inside or outside.

See security products

Shading and sun control

Roll shutters and roll screens provide excellent sun and temperature control without permanently blocking your view. Shading keeps your house, patio or deck cool and saves energy year round.

See sun control and shading products

  • Energy savings

    Solar shade fabrics block 75 to 90 percent of the sun's rays before they reach the glass. In winter the same systems cut heat loss, so cooling and heating bills both come down.

    Energy saving products

  • Privacy

    Roll shutters, Bahama shutters and privacy screens let you close off a bedroom, lanai or storefront in seconds, then open it back up to the view when you want it.

    Privacy products

Why Alutech

Engineered so the housing gets smaller and the protection does not.

Our patented end retention system eliminates storm bars entirely. The result is a smoother, quieter shutter with a tighter roll diameter, so the housing above your window shrinks while the system still exceeds the toughest building codes in the world.

  • End retention, no storm bars

    Stainless steel retention bolts lock every slat into the side rails. Nothing to store, nothing to forget when the storm is coming.

  • Any shape, any size

    Roll shutters up to 24 feet wide and 20 feet tall, screens up to 25 feet, and asymmetric shapes we have built since the Studio Star shutter in 1984.

  • Manual, motorized or automated

    Hand crank, Somfy and Hammerhead motors, remote control and home automation on every roll shutter and screen we make.

  • Tested to the toughest codes

    Florida Building Code including HVHZ, Miami-Dade, Texas Department of Insurance, IBC and IRC. Approved for block, concrete and wood frame construction.

Company

A family engineering firm, fifty years on.

Alutech United, Inc. is a leading manufacturer of roll shutters and roll screens. For more than 25 years in the United States we have taken great care in delivering the highest quality products to our customers, and we believe in setting standards of quality and service that our competitors are yet to match.

Being a mechanical engineer, I was looking for a niche product and discovered new applications for roll shutters.

Hans Schanz, founder
  1. 1971

    Beginnings in Germany

    Hans Schanz sets out to develop roll shutters significantly different from anything on the market.

  2. 1984

    Building nationally

    The Studio Star roll shutter conforms to almost any window shape. Within a few years the company leads the market for asymmetric roll shutters.

  3. 1990s

    Expanding internationally

    Holger Schanz incorporates WIGA GmbH for Northern Europe. Joe Schanz grows the American subsidiary and today is president of Alutech United in Delaware.

  4. Today

    Alutech United

    One of the most diverse product lines in the North American shutter market, from Barracuda and Magnum shutters to Storm Tex and Zip Tex screens.
