For homeowners

Why the best shutter is the one a dealer installs.

Hurricane protection only works if it is measured, anchored and finished correctly. That is why Alutech sells through factory trained dealers rather than a shopping cart.

  • Measured for your opening, not the other way around

    Arched, angled, oversized or wrapped around a balcony. Your dealer measures every opening so the housing, tracks and slats fit your home. We have built asymmetric shutters since the Studio Star in 1984.

  • Anchored to the tested approval

    A hurricane rating depends on the fasteners and the wall they go into. Dealers install to the approvals for block, concrete and wood frame construction under the Florida Building Code, Miami-Dade, TDI and IBC, and handle permits where they are required.

  • Motors set, remotes paired, automation connected

    Somfy and Hammerhead motors need their limits set and their remotes programmed. Your dealer does it on site and shows you how to run the system before the crew leaves.

  • Warranty and a phone number that answers

    Dealer installed systems are backed by the Alutech warranty and by a local business that services what it sells. When a storm is a week out, that matters more than the price tag.

Why Alutech

Engineered so the housing gets smaller and the protection does not.

Our patented end retention system eliminates storm bars entirely. The result is a smoother, quieter shutter with a tighter roll diameter, so the housing above your window shrinks while the system still exceeds the toughest building codes in the world. For dealers, that means fewer parts and faster installs. For homeowners, it means a cleaner look and nothing to store in the garage.

  • End retention, no storm bars

    Stainless steel retention bolts lock every slat into the side rails. Nothing to store, nothing to forget when the storm is coming.

  • Any shape, any size

    Roll shutters up to 24 feet wide and 20 feet tall, screens up to 25 feet, and asymmetric shapes we have built since the Studio Star shutter in 1984.

  • Manual, motorized or automated

    Hand crank, Somfy and Hammerhead motors, remote control and home automation on every roll shutter and screen we make.

  • Tested to the toughest codes

    Florida Building Code including HVHZ, Miami-Dade, Texas Department of Insurance, IBC and IRC. Approved for block, concrete and wood frame construction.

Company

A family engineering firm, fifty years on.

Alutech United, Inc. is a leading manufacturer of roll shutters and roll screens. For more than 25 years in the United States we have taken great care in delivering the highest quality products to our customers, and we believe in setting standards of quality and service that our competitors are yet to match.

Being a mechanical engineer, I was looking for a niche product and discovered new applications for roll shutters.

Hans Schanz, founder
  1. 1971

    Beginnings in Germany

    Hans Schanz sets out to develop roll shutters significantly different from anything on the market.

  2. 1984

    Building nationally

    The Studio Star roll shutter conforms to almost any window shape. Within a few years the company leads the market for asymmetric roll shutters.

  3. 1990s

    Expanding internationally

    Holger Schanz incorporates WIGA GmbH for Northern Europe. Joe Schanz grows the American subsidiary and today is president of Alutech United in Delaware.

  4. Today

    Alutech United

    One of the most diverse product lines in the North American shutter market, sold and installed through a network of more than 500 trained dealers.
